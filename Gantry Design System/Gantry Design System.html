01

Mark & wordmark

An abstract “h”-ligature: two vertical strokes joined by a diagonal crossbar. Three approved treatments. The mark never rotates, never gains a gradient, and clear space equals the width of one stroke on all sides.

Typography

Space Grotesk for display headings — wide, geometric, structural, echoing the wordmark. Inter for body and UI. JetBrains Mono for everything machine-measured — tokens, costs, latency, IDs. Tabular figures everywhere numbers align. No serifs, no editorial display.
